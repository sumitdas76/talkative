## Intent

A short promo announcing Talkative (formerly Sumit Speak), a Windows
hold-to-talk local dictation app, and highlighting its headline features.
Confident and direct, not gimmicky — the app's own real identity is
already privacy-forward and no-nonsense, so the video should read the
same way. Hook on the privacy/cost angle other dictation tools don't
offer (Wispr Flow, Willow Voice — cloud-based, $12-15/mo), then move
through the headline features, close on the rename and the logo.

Beats: hook (every other dictation app sends your voice to the cloud) →
turn (Talkative doesn't) → proof (100% offline, 100% private, free, no
subscription) → features (Voice to Text, Cleaned Up Mode, Auto Text &
Dictionary, Dictation History) → rename beat (same app, same developer,
now called Talkative) → close (logo + tagline).

## Notes

- No live product UI to capture — Talkative is a native Windows tray app
  (Tkinter + system tray), not a website, so there is nothing to crawl or
  screenshot. All visuals are invented (motion graphics, icons, on-screen
  text), not real screen capture. Use the no-capture path.
- Real, verified facts to draw on (do not invent product claims beyond
  these): 100% offline speech-to-text (faster-whisper) and grammar
  cleanup (local small LLM) — no cloud calls except the one-time model
  download and a daily update/feedback check; free, no subscription;
  hold-to-talk hotkey (configurable, 1-2 keys); "Cleaned up" mode removes
  filler words, spoken corrections, and near-repeats, and fixes grammar;
  personal dictionary (spoken word/phrase → typed text, e.g. "ASAP" →
  "as soon as possible"); opt-in local-only dictation history, off by
  default; formerly named Sumit Speak, renamed to Talkative 2026-08-23,
  same developer, settings/models/history carry over automatically.
- Competitor framing for the hook (from prior research, do not overstate):
  Wispr Flow and Willow Voice are cloud-based dictation tools with
  monthly/annual subscriptions ($12-15/mo range); Talkative is local-only
  and free by contrast. Keep the comparison to this one clear contrast,
  not a detailed feature-by-feature takedown.
